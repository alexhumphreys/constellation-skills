# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 3 June 2026*

Constellation Skills is developed by Alex Humphreys (Berlin, Germany).
For privacy questions, contact humphreys.a@gmail.com.

## What we collect

Nothing. Constellation Skills does not collect, store, or transmit any
personal data to servers we operate, and we do not use analytics,
advertising identifiers, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Where your data lives

- **On your device.** All canvas content — skills, attachments, notes,
  and layouts — is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad.
- **In your iCloud account, if you enable it.** Sync uses Apple's
  CloudKit service to store data in your private iCloud container.
  We have no access to it. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple                                                                                                            handles iCloud data.
- **Between your own devices on the same Wi-Fi network.** Local-network
  sync uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework to exchange data
  directly between your devices. Traffic is encrypted by the framework                                                                                                         and does not pass through any server we operate.

## Device permissions

The app requests access to the camera (for scanning pairing QR codes),
the photo library (for attaching photos and videos to skills), and the
local network (for device-to-device sync). You can change these in iOS
Settings at any time.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to
access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You can remove all
locally stored app data by deleting the app, and manage iCloud storage
via Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage.
