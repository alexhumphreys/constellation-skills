# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 16 September 2026*

Constellation Skills is developed by Alex Humphreys (Berlin, Germany).
For privacy questions, contact hello@alexhumphreys.me.

## What we collect

Nothing. Constellation Skills does not collect, store, or transmit any
personal data to servers we operate, and we do not use analytics,
advertising identifiers, or third-party tracking SDKs. There are no
accounts and no sign-in.

## Where your data lives

- **On your device.** All canvas content — skills, attachments, notes,
  and layouts — is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad.
- **In your iCloud account, if you enable it.** Sync uses Apple's
  CloudKit service to store data in your private iCloud container. We
  have no access to it. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles
  iCloud data. The app works fully with iCloud switched off, in which
  case your data stays on the device.

## Device permissions

The app asks for access to your photo library, so you can attach photos
and videos to a skill. It reads only the items you pick, stores them in
the app's own storage, and never sends them to us or to anyone else. You
can change this in iOS Settings at any time.

That is the only permission the app requests.

## Links you save

Links you save to a skill are stored alongside your other data. Opening
one leaves the app and hands you to that website, which has its own
privacy policy and is not covered by this one.

## Diagnostics

The app keeps a diagnostic log to help it recover from errors. It is
written to your device, is never uploaded, and is removed when you
delete the app.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to
access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You can remove all
locally stored app data by deleting the app, and manage iCloud storage
via Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will appear at
this URL with a new "Last updated" date. If a future version of the app
ever collects personal data, or requests a permission not listed above,
this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before
that change takes effect.
